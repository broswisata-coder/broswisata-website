Route Planning

Singapore to North Sumatra

Private North Sumatra tour planning for Singapore travelers flying SIN-KNO: Medan arrival, Lake Toba, Bukit Lawang, Berastagi, solo or small-group quotation.

Plan the first and final night

  • Arrival time at Kualanamu affects whether the first night should be in Medan or outside the city.
  • Solo and small-group trips need transport, room, and guide arrangements suited to the actual group.
  • Final drop-off can be Medan city hotel when the guest arranges an extra night.
  • For an agreed Wise or bank transfer payment, keep the booking confirmation and receipt together so the recorded amount and payment terms are clear.

Check airport pickup and flight scope

  • Send the flight number, arrival date, local landing time, guests, and luggage. Pickup planning also needs time for airport formalities, baggage, and the agreed meeting point; landing time is not the same as road departure time.
  • Ask whether the arrival day can support the proposed road transfer or needs a Medan night first. For the return, check the final overnight base and airport transfer against the booked flight.
  • A ground-tour quotation and an international flight booking are separate arrangements unless explicitly stated otherwise. Check exactly which transport, hotels, meals, guides, and activities the written quote covers.
  • Tell Ahmad about any flights, hotel nights, or activities you book yourself so the private route can be checked around them.

Good route lengths

  • 4D3N for Bukit Lawang or Lake Toba focus.
  • 5D4N for Berastagi and Lake Toba with a better pace.
  • 6D5N for Bukit Lawang, Berastagi, Lake Toba, and Samosir.
  • Custom trip for solo female travelers who want clear guide and transport coordination.

Before you request a quote

  • Share flight number and arrival time.
  • Share whether you prefer comfort, eco lodge, or simple local stay.
  • Tell us if final night in Medan is self-arranged.
  • Share hiking fitness and must-see places.

FAQ

Questions before planning your trip

Check the route, trip length, and arrangements before asking Ahmad for a private quotation.

Can BROS Wisata handle one-pax Singapore guests?

Yes. One-pax private trips are possible with a custom quote and clear ground arrangement.

Can I pay 50% deposit first?

For confirmed bookings, deposit and balance terms can be stated in the booking confirmation and payment receipt.

Can the balance be paid during the trip?

It can be arranged when agreed in writing before the trip, for example during the Lake Toba or Samosir stay.

Does the North Sumatra quotation include flights from Singapore?

Do not assume international flights are included. Check the written quotation for its exact ground services and any separately agreed flight arrangements, then share the booked flight details for pickup and drop-off planning.
