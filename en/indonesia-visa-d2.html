What is this route for?

For business discussions, meetings, negotiations or visit activities consistent with the permission granted. Explain travel frequency, receiving companies and activities on each visit, rather than only requesting the longest validity.

Entry type & permitted stay

Multiple entry. Multiple entry allows repeat arrivals under the visa conditions granted. Visa validity differs from the stay limit on each arrival; Ms Yana must confirm both separately.

Important limits

  • A price labelled per year is not permission to stay continuously for a year.
  • D2 is not permission for routine employment, receiving wages or continuous production supervision.
  • Activity changes and extension needs require separate review; approval is not guaranteed.

Document preparation

This is initial preparation guidance, not a final checklist for every applicant. Ms Yana checks nationality, activities, guarantor requirements and applicable documents before submission.

  • Travel document and a recent photograph for the initial review.
  • Repeat-visit plans, locations and your business relationship with the receiving organisation.
  • An invitation or company statement, itinerary and evidence of financial means may be requested.
  • Guarantor needs and the final checklist are reviewed by nationality and application route.

Privacy & visa documents

Do not upload passports or bank statements through general website forms. Confirm the recipient, intended use and delivery channel before sharing documents.

Before you apply

Am I automatically eligible?

No. Nationality, travel documents, actual activities, guarantor requirements and current status need review. This page provides initial information, not immigration approval.

Does this price include every fee?

The displayed amount is a Bros reference service rate. Components, scope, payment terms and the applicable price are explained in a written quotation before payment.

Are processing and approval guaranteed?

No. Estimates depend on the category, complete documents and official review. BROS assists with administration, not the visa or entry decision.

Can I carry out activities beyond the visa purpose?

Your activities must match the permission granted. Work and remuneration rules vary by category; explain your real role and activities for review before applying.
